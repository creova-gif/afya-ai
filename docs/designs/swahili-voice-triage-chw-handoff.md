## Problem Statement
In Tanzania and broader East Africa, patients face acute friction when seeking timely health triage: long dispensary wait times, linguistic barriers with technical jargon, and disconnected self-diagnosis tools. When individuals fall sick or manage chronic conditions, they often delay care or lack a coherent history of symptoms. Simultaneously, Community Health Workers (CHWs) and rural clinic nurses spend excessive manual effort piecing together fragmented patient narratives under high patient volume.

## What Makes This Cool
A patient speaks or texts freely in conversational Swahili or Sheng about how they feel ("Kichwa kinanigonga sana toka asubuhi na ninahisi baridi kali"). Afya AI doesn't just reply with a canned disclaimer; it conducts an intelligent, empathetic bilingual triage interview adhering to East African clinical guidelines (malaria, typhoid, maternal red flags, hypertension). 

The "whoa" moment: with one tap, Afya AI generates a dual-output:
1. **Patient Care Card**: Direct, reassuring guidance in plain Swahili on immediate self-care, hydration, and danger signs to monitor.
2. **Clinician Handoff Note (SBAR/SOAP format)**: A dense, formatted medical summary ready to transmit via WhatsApp, SMS, or direct export to the local dispensary or CHW, cutting nurse triage intake time from 15 minutes to 30 seconds.

## Constraints
- **Low Bandwidth / High Latency**: Audio and payload sizes must be optimized for 2G/3G connectivity.
- **Language Nuance**: Tanzanian Swahili idioms, phonetic spellings, and mixed Swahili-English (Sheng) require robust multimodal AI parsing.
- **Clinical Safety Guardrails**: Zero hallucinations on prescription dosage; explicit emergency red-flag escalation (e.g. maternal bleeding, severe dehydration, respiratory distress).
- **Privacy & Anonymity**: Compliant with regional data governance and patient trust standards; local anonymization of PII before any cloud LLM calls.

## Premises
1. **Vernacular & Cultural Grounding**: Generic health bots fail without East African dietary, linguistic, and disease-pattern context.
2. **Community Health Workers as the Force Multiplier**: The platform must bridge patients to real human care providers rather than pretending to replace doctors.
3. **Dual Patient-Family-Clinic Architecture**: Health in Africa is communal; linking patient symptoms to family records and clinic handoffs creates sustainable adherence.

## Approaches Considered
### Approach A: Voice-First Swahili Triage & CHW SOAP Note Handoff (Selected)
Direct end-to-end symptom interview via audio or text in Swahili, generating both a plain-language patient summary and a structured clinical triage note for CHWs.
### Approach B: Family Health Circles & East African Meal Vision Tracking
A daily engagement model focusing on step tracking, shared family wellness rings, and computer vision recognition of local dishes (Ugali, Sukuma, Samaki). Deferred to Milestone 2 to prioritize life-saving clinical triage.

## Recommended Approach
Approach A provides the highest immediate utility, clearest clinical impact, and most compelling demonstration for community clinics and open-source health partners.

## Architecture & Data Flow
1. **Audio/Text Ingestion**: Web Audio API recording or text input in React component.
2. **Transcription & Multimodal LLM Reasoning**: Swahili audio/text routed through localized prompt schemas incorporating clinical triage protocols.
3. **Structured SBAR / SOAP Output**:
   - `Subjective`: Chief complaint, onset, symptom timeline in patient's words.
   - `Objective`: Reported vitals (temperature, heart rate, blood pressure if logged).
   - `Assessment`: Stratified risk level (Green = Home care / monitor; Yellow = Schedule CHW visit; Red = Immediate clinic emergency).
   - `Plan`: Patient instructions + clinician handoff checklist.
4. **Handoff Dispatch**: WhatsApp deep link (`https://wa.me/?text=...`), clipboard copy, or local PDF export.

## Success Criteria
- Patient can complete a 3-turn Swahili symptom triage in under 90 seconds.
- Clinical note accurately captures chief complaints, duration, and red flags in standardized SOAP format.
- Works reliably in demo mode with pre-seeded scenarios (Malaria, Maternal wellness, Hypertension).
- Clean handoff export link formatted for WhatsApp sharing to CHWs.
